# Attribution and license status

The research uses Qwen3.5-0.8B, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers and PEFT, with their original authors and terms. Library implementations and pretrained model/tokenizer weights are not copied into this candidate. Imports, model identifiers and recorded versions remain for reproducibility. LoRA, causal language-model training and supervised fine-tuning are existing methods; this repository does not claim their invention.

Scientific paper text, original private data, raw model generations and private adapter stores are excluded. Released examples are hand-written fictional parser cases; released article-level evidence consists of counts/hashes and research metadata, not an openly licensed article dataset. The owner must provide authorized data and satisfy the pretrained model's terms when restoring training or distributing adapters.

The original task-output code folders have no project-wide license file. The owner has not selected an open-source license for research-specific scripts or explanatory materials. No permissive blanket license is assigned by this preparation. See [LICENSE_PENDING.md](LICENSE_PENDING.md).
